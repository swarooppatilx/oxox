Noughts & Crosses

Solo Online

 

 

Play Tic Tac Toe on notebook paper against Jev, an AI model, or challenge a friend to a live online match.

You play crosses against Jev, an AI model, in a five-game match on a sheet of notebook paper. If Jev is unavailable, a minimax bot plays instead. You can also play a friend online. Turn on JavaScript to play.
